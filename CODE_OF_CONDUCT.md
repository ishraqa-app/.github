# Code of Conduct

Ishraqa is a community for learning and building together. In every repository, issue and discussion:

- Be kind and respectful. Critique ideas, never people.
- Help newcomers; every expert was once a beginner.
- No harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Keep private information private.

Maintainers may remove content or contributors that break these rules. Report concerns to hello@ishraqa.app.
